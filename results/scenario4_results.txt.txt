**Scenario 4: Heavy Traffic Simulation Results**

**Project:** SUMO-Integrated Edge-IoT Based Adaptive Multi-Junction Traffic Signal Preemption for Emergency Ambulance

**Simulation tool:** SUMO 1.27.1 with Python and TraCI

**Scenario setup:**
- Background traffic flows in forward and reverse directions.
- Traffic generation period: 2 seconds per direction.
- Two ambulances were simulated.
- Forward ambulance departure time: 10 seconds.
- Reverse ambulance departure time: 70 seconds.

**Observed results:**

| Parameter | Forward ambulance | Reverse ambulance |
|---|---:|---:|
| Departure time | 10 s | 70 s |
| Arrival time | 104 s | 195 s |
| Travel time | 94 s | 125 s |

**Additional observation:** The simulation contained up to 165 vehicles at the observed 181-second timestamp.

**Conclusion:** Both ambulances completed their routes under simulated heavy-traffic conditions. A separate simulation without ambulance signal preemption is required to calculate the travel-time reduction achieved by the proposed system.

**Note:** These are prototype simulation results, not real-road performance measurements.
